The core

The Arbiter, at the heart of Delest

A typical cleaner deletes whatever looks like a cache. Delest does the opposite: it only proposes what it can prove rebuilds, and it shows you the proof.

Only what rebuilds

target, node_modules, build, .gradle, .venv, Pods, .next: recognised by their manifest and lockfile, never by their name alone.

Every verdict explained

Ignored by git, next to its manifest, last commit three days ago: every proposal comes with its reasons.

Absolute bans

Work git does not track, tracked files, secrets (.env, .pem), source code and Docker volumes are never proposed.

Nothing leaves without you

A preview, your consent, then the quarantine: seven days to restore anything, before the final deletion.

Integration

With your agents

At the start of each session, Delest looks at the free space. It stays quiet, unless the disk is filling up.

In the session

At most one alert a day and one notice a week: in the agent's session, and as a system notification.

Or every four hours

An optional timer, off by default, for the work done without an agent: launchd, systemd or the Task Scheduler.

An MCP tool

Your agents can ask for the disk's state and the reasons for a verdict, read-only.

Local AI

AI that stays on your machine

For folders no rule recognises, Delest asks a small local model for an opinion. It can only make Delest more careful.

It recognises

EmbeddingGemma 2 matches an unknown folder with known decisions and rules.

It reasons and answers

Gemma 4 E2B gives its opinion and answers your questions about a folder. It runs only when memory and battery allow.

It never decides

The model never makes a folder deletable and lifts no ban. On a click, you can also put the question to your agent.

Privacy

Your code stays with you

Delest reads folder names and facts (size, git state, nearby manifests). It does not open your files to send them anywhere.

  • No account, no ads, no analytics.
  • The AI models run on your machine, offline.
  • “Ask the agent” sends only the folder's path and the names of what it holds, and only when you click.
  • Everything Delest does is written to a journal, on your machine.

FAQ

Questions

Does Delest delete anything on its own?

No. It proposes, you choose, then the folders go into the quarantine for seven days. Nothing is deleted without your consent.

What if I need a quarantined folder back?

Restore it from the Quarantine page, or with delest restore, within seven days. After that, it is deleted.

Do I need an AI agent to use Delest?

No. The app and the command work on their own; agents only add a check at each session.

What does Delest do with a folder it does not recognise?

It does not propose it. The local model can give an opinion, always towards caution, and the decision is yours.

Does Delest download AI models on its own?

No. Delest works without them. In Settings › AI, “Install the local AI” shows what it would download (about 3.2 GB), and fetches it only after you confirm; each file is checked against its published fingerprint. The engine needs macOS 13.3 or later, and on Windows a recent Visual C++ runtime.

How much does Delest cost?

Nothing. Delest is free and open source, under the MIT licence: its code is on GitHub.
