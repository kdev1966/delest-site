What Delest deletes, and your decisions

Delest deletes nothing without your consent. It proposes folders, you choose them, then they go into the quarantine for seven days before being deleted; “Delete permanently” erases them at once. Read the preview before confirming: a folder may take time to rebuild (a full build) or need the network (dependencies to download again). Keep backups of your work: Delest is not one.
